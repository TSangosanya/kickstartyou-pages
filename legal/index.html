Privacy

Our contact details

KickStartYou is run by Toluwani Sangosanya, who is responsible for how your personal information is used.
Email: toluwani@kickstartyou.org

The information we collect

We collect the following information when you give it to us:

  • Your first name and email address
  • Whether you are an international student
  • Whether you identify as Black (you may choose "Prefer not to say")
  • Your current stage of study
  • Any further details you choose to share, such as the sectors and roles you are interested in, your city, your home country, your university, the challenges you face and how you heard about us
  • Which options you choose in our newsletter polls

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Send you The Weekly Kick, our weekly newsletter
  • Understand who we are reaching, so we can improve our roles tracker, newsletter and website
  • Shape future services around what our community needs

Our lawful basis

We only use your information with your consent. You give it when you sign up or answer our questions.

Information about whether you identify as Black is ethnic origin data, which the law treats as a special category of personal data. We only collect it with your explicit consent, and you can always choose "Prefer not to say".

You can withdraw your consent at any time by unsubscribing from any of our emails or by contacting us. This does not affect anything we did with your information before you withdrew it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we delete your details within 12 months. We may keep a record of your email address only to make sure you are not sent emails again.

Who else handles it

We use trusted services to run KickStartYou. They only process your information on our behalf:

  • Brevo: sends The Weekly Kick and stores subscriber details
  • Google: Google Analytics, which counts visits and clicks on our website (only if you accept cookies)
  • Microsoft: Microsoft Clarity, which shows how people use our website (only if you accept cookies), and Microsoft Forms and OneDrive, which hold answers from our earlier sign-up form
  • GitHub: hosts our website

Some of these services may store information outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

Sharing your information

We will never sell your personal information.

In the future, we may share overall figures about our community with partners, for example "most of our readers are in their final year". These figures never include your name, email address or anything else that could identify you.

We may also share information if the law requires us to.

Newsletter polls

When you choose an option in one of our newsletter polls, Brevo records which option you chose. We use this to understand what our readers need. We only report poll results as totals.

Cookies

Our website only uses cookies if you accept them. You can read more in our Cookie policy.

Keeping your information safe

We use trusted services with strong security, and only Toluwani has access to your personal information.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Ask us to limit how we use your information
  • Object to how we use your information
  • Ask for your information in a format you can take to another organisation
  • Withdraw your consent at any time

You do not have to pay to use these rights. To make a request, email toluwani@kickstartyou.org.

How to complain

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please contact us first at toluwani@kickstartyou.org and we will do our best to put it right.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, with the date it was last updated shown at the top.

Terms

Not immigration advice

The roles, news and information on this website and in The Weekly Kick are for general information only. They are not immigration or legal advice. For advice about your own situation, check gov.uk or speak to a regulated immigration adviser.

We are not the employer

We find roles on public job boards and employer websites. We are not the employer and are not involved in any hiring decisions.

A sponsor licence means an employer can sponsor a visa. It does not guarantee that any particular role will be sponsored. Always check the job advert and the employer's own website before applying.

Accuracy

We check every role before we add it, but details such as closing dates can change after we publish them. We cannot guarantee that all information is complete or up to date.

Links to other websites

Our website links to employers and other websites. We are not responsible for their content or how they handle your information.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Cookie policy

What cookies are

Cookies are small files saved on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember information about your visit.

The cookies we use

We use two optional tools that set cookies:

  • Google Analytics, which counts visits and shows which pages and roles are clicked most
  • Microsoft Clarity, which shows how visitors scroll and move around our pages

We use them to improve our website. They do not tell us who you are.

They only run if you choose "Accept" on our cookie banner. If you choose "Reject", no cookies are set.

Remembering your choices

Our website also remembers whether you have accepted or rejected cookies and whether you have closed the sign-up pop-up, so we do not ask you again. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Changing your mind

You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.
