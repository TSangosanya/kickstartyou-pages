Meet the founder

Hi, this is Toluwani.

Toluwani studied Cybersecurity and Ethical Hacking at university. As a student ambassador she saw up close the challenges international students face. She later watched friends graduate with first-class degrees and still struggle in their job search, because they required an employer with a sponsor licence to provide a work visa. Driven by a refusal to watch talented individuals continue to miss out, she took action.

Currently pursuing a master's program in Spain, she is versed in the realities of studying abroad and possesses an understanding of the challenges and rewards international students experience. With the current challenges and highly competitive nature of the job market, requiring visa sponsorship adds a significant layer of difficulty to the search.

Finding a job on a visa takes a lot of steps. KickStartYou is here to make one of those steps easier.

About KickStartYou

Two barriers. One place that looks at both.

International students from the African and Caribbean diaspora in the UK face two barriers at once: finding an employer that can sponsor a visa, and racial bias in hiring. Visa platforms ignore race. Black career platforms ignore visas. KickStartYou offers free career support that looks at both.
